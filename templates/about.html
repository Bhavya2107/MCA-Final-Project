01 Our Story

Built on trust, driven by technology

Founded in 2024, COMPUTER & CCTV HUB started with a simple mission: to provide customers with access to quality laptops and security systems at competitive prices. What began as a small venture has grown into a trusted platform serving thousands of customers across Ahmedabad and beyond.

We believe that everyone deserves access to reliable computing devices and security solutions without breaking the bank. Whether you're looking for the latest flagship laptop, a budget-friendly refurbished device, or a complete CCTV setup — we've got you covered.

Our Mission

To democratize access to quality technology by offering the best selection of laptops and CCTV systems at competitive prices with exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted and customer-centric technology marketplace, known for quality, reliability, and exceptional after-sales support.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, and customer satisfaction are at the core of everything we do. We're committed to ethical practices and continuous improvement.
